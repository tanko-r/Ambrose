§ 6.4 — Hazardous Materials
To Seller's actual knowledge, no Hazardous Materials have been released or disposed of on the Property in violation of any Environmental Law; Seller has received no written notice from any Governmental Authority of any investigation or enforcement proceeding relating to Hazardous Materials on or affecting the Property.
⚠  HIGH: Written Notice Trap
§ 8.2 — Indemnification
Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer from and against any and all losses, costs, claims, damages, liabilities and expenses of any kind or nature whatsoever arising from or relating to Seller's breach of any representation or warranty.
⚠  HIGH: Uncapped Liability
§ 12.2 — Seller Default
Upon Seller's default, Buyer shall have the right to terminate and receive return of the Earnest Money, or to seek specific performance, or to pursue all remedies available at law and in equity including consequential and punitive damages.
⚠  HIGH: Consequential Damages
§ 6.4 — No Phase I Required
The representation regarding underground storage tanks and prior contamination is made solely on Seller's actual knowledge with no obligation to commission or deliver a Phase I or Phase II Environmental Site Assessment to Buyer at or prior to Closing.
◆  MEDIUM: No Phase I Obligation
§ 5.4 — Closing Conditions
Buyer's obligation to close is conditioned upon satisfaction of all conditions precedent in Buyer's sole and absolute discretion, with no stated timeline for waiver or exercise, and no obligation on Buyer to promptly notify Seller of any unsatisfied condition.
◆  OPPORTUNITY: Add Cure Period

6.2  No Conflicts.  Neither the execution and delivery of this Agreement nor the performance of Seller's obligations hereunder will (i) conflict with or result in a breach of Seller's organizational documents, or (ii) violate any agreement, order, or applicable law to which Seller is a party or bound.
6.3  Litigation.  To Seller's actual knowledge, there are no actions, suits, or proceedings pending or threatened against Seller or the Property before any court or governmental authority that would materially affect the Property or Seller's ability to perform its obligations under this Agreement.
6.4  Hazardous Materials.  To Seller's actual knowledge, (i) no Hazardous Materials have been released, discharged, disposed of, transported, generated, or stored in, on, under, or about the Property in violation of any applicable Environmental Law; (ii) no underground storage tanks are located on or under the Property; (iii) Seller has received no written notice from any Governmental Authority of any investigation, request for information, notice of violation, or enforcement proceeding relating to Hazardous Materials on or affecting the Property; and (iv) to Seller's knowledge, the Property is currently in material compliance with all applicable Environmental Laws. As used herein, "Hazardous Materials" means any substance defined or regulated as hazardous, toxic, or a pollutant under applicable Environmental Laws.

§6.4(iii) limits Seller's rep to formal written notice from regulators. Seller may have received oral regulatory contacts, attended informal site visits, or have direct knowledge of a pending enforcement action — all without formal written notice — and still technically satisfy this representation. A classic drafter's trap that leaves Buyer exposed.
