# Product Definition - Ambrose

## Initial Concept
Ambrose is an AI-powered collaborative contract review tool designed specifically for law firm attorneys and in-house counsel. It aims to significantly improve attorney work product by accelerating the redlining process and increasing the accuracy of risk detection.

## Target Users
- **Law Firm Attorneys:** From associates to partners who need to review and redline contracts efficiently while maintaining high standards.
- **In-House Counsel:** Legal teams within corporations managing a high volume of contracts who need to identify risks and align documents with company standards quickly.

## Goals & Objectives
- **Accelerate Redlining:** Leverage AI to generate surgical redlines, reducing the time spent on manual drafting.
- **Improve Risk Detection:** Utilize large language models (Claude and Gemini) to identify legal exposure and opportunities across various contract types.
- **Enhance Work Product:** Provide faster output without sacrificing the quality or precision required in legal practice.
- **Optimize Communications:** Improve attorney note-taking and streamline communication with clients regarding contract risks and changes.

## Core Features
- **High-Fidelity Document Viewer:** A viewer that faithfully preserves the original Word document formatting for a familiar and professional experience.
- **Context-Aware AI Revisions:** Revision generation that incorporates precedent documents, specific deal context, and lawyer-defined review guidelines.
- **Improved Note-Taking & Client Comms:** Integrated tools for attorneys to capture notes and generate transmittal summaries for clients.
- **Dynamic Risk Navigation:** An interactive interface that allows users to navigate documents based on AI-identified risks, conceptual categories, and severity.

## Visual Identity & Design Principles
- **Professional and Trustworthy:** A clean, "Vercel-like" aesthetic that conveys precision and reliability suitable for legal professional use.
- **Layered Information Density:** The interface maintains a focused appearance but provides high density through "hidden" UI elements. Information is layered via popups and side panels, allowing attorneys to pull in the specific data they need for a given project or moment without cluttering the primary view.
